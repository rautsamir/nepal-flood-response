METHODOLOGY

How locations are ranked

acute need = damaged buildings × population
priority = acute need × (1 + 0.5 × damaged bridges within 5 km)

Buildings are grouped into approximately 1 km cells and joined to a population grid. Nearby damaged bridges increase the priority score as an access constraint. Helipad distance is shown as context but does not change the score.

These are relative triage indicators, not counts of casualties, displaced people or verified unmet need.
