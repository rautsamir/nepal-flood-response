Open data. Faster decisions.

UNFRC turns scattered flood data into a ranked, mapped picture of where help may be needed most.

Why it exists

During a flood, damage, population, and access information often sit in separate datasets. UNFRC joins those signals into one triage view and makes it accessible through plain-language questions.
